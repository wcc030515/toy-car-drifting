Race Start Countdown by kheetor, used under the CC0 option on the original page:
https://opengameart.org/content/race-start-countdown
Ticking clock. by bart, CC0:
https://opengameart.org/content/ticking-clock
The race recording is split at 1 / 3 seconds to synchronize each cue with the
game clock. Four original clock ticks alternate during a bomb's fuse, with
increasing repetition speed and no artificial pitch rise. Samples are converted
to mono PCM WAV with light low-pass filtering, peak 0.60 and soft edge fades.
See LICENSE-countdowns-CC0.txt. Publisher pages verified 2026-11-05.

Background music: Pure Raceway (normal version, BPM 160) by MintoDog.
Published on OpenGameArt, explicitly marked CC0:
https://opengameart.org/content/pure-raceway
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Publisher pages and licenses verified 2026-11-04.

The original music MP3 is unchanged. Gameplay effect recordings were decoded to PCM WAV,
peak normalized to 0.80 with headroom for device resampling; the two looping
effects use a 40 ms overlap crossfade. One-shot edges fade over 1.5 / 4 ms.
The game applies gain, panning, pitch and playback envelopes at runtime.
No replacement synthesized interaction sound effects were generated.
Original reference-game engine and skid audio remain separate. Collision playback
keeps the reference game's speed-dependent gain, tone and spatialization, with
the selected Kenney recordings replacing its generated impact buffers.
The three v9.8 UI recordings replace the older interface clicks. They use a
two-stage 1800 Hz low-pass, peak 0.42 and 5 / 18 ms edge fades for softer feedback.
